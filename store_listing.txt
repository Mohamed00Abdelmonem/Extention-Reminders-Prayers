Taqwa – Prayer Times, Azkar & Quran Radio is a lightweight Chrome extension that helps you stay consistent with your daily prayers.

Get accurate prayer times based on your location, smart Adhan notifications, morning & evening Azkar, and listen to live Quran radio — all in one clean and distraction-free interface.

Features:

* Accurate prayer times (auto location or manual city selection)
* Adhan alerts before and at prayer time
* Daily Azkar (morning & evening)
* Quran radio streaming
* Clean UI with dark & light mode

Stay connected to your faith every day with Taqwa.
